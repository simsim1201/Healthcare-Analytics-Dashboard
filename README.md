Hospital Readmission Risk Analysis

Designed and developed a multi-page Healthcare Performance Dashboard analyzing revenue, patient demographics, and provider efficiency.
Built interactive filters for Doctor, Hospital, Insurance Provider, and Admission Date.
Created KPIs including Total Revenue (1.42bn), Patient Count (40K+), Hospital and Doctor performance metrics.
Performed trend analysis and YoY revenue evaluation using DAX time intelligence functions.
Identified top revenue-generating hospitals and insurance providers to support financial analysis.
Enhanced dashboard usability with slicers, dynamic visuals, and executive-level summary cards.

Business Problem Statement:-
Healthcare organizations generate large volumes of patient, hospital, and billing data. However, decision-makers often lack a centralized system to monitor revenue performance, patient demographics, admission patterns, and provider efficiency in a single interactive view.
Project Objective:-
The objective of this project was to design a comprehensive Healthcare Dashboard in Power BI that enables stakeholders to analyze patient distribution, revenue performance, hospital efficiency, insurance provider contribution, and doctor-level performance over time.
Business Insights Derived:-
Revenue Insights
Total Revenue: 1.42bn
Insurance providers like Cigna and Medicare contribute significantly to revenue.
Revenue shows strong growth from 2019-2023 before decline in 2024 (possible operational or data cut-off issue).
Operational Insights:-
Top hospitals generate over 1M in revenue.
Certain doctors handle higher patient volumes while generating higher billing amounts.
Admission types (Elective, Urgent, Emergency) are almost evenly distributed, indicating balanced case mix.
Patient Insights:-
Gender distribution is nearly equal.
Middle-aged and older patients form a larger share.
Blood type and medical condition distribution highlight common treatment categories.
